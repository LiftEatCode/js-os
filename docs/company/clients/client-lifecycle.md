# Client Lifecycle
**Status:** Design v1

## Lifecycle
**Prospect → Won → Onboarding → Baseline → Growth Plan → Execution → Measurement → Review → Retention/Expansion → Offboarding**

## Won
Record scope, terms, start date, responsibilities, and onboarding work.

## Onboarding
Collect business context, goals, priority services, service areas, brand assets, analytics, website/deployment access, GBP access where applicable, and communication preferences.

## Baseline
Capture enough evidence to compare future performance without overstating causality.

## Growth Plan
Prioritize by impact, confidence, effort, risk, urgency, and dependencies.

## Execution
Use controlled work items and approvals for consequential actions.

## Review
Explain what changed, why, results/learning, current metrics, next priorities, and blockers.

## Expansion
Expand only where an adjacent capability has a clear business case.

## Offboarding
Revoke/transfer access appropriately, document final state, preserve required audit history, and follow data-retention rules.
