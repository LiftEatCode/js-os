# Client Retention and Expansion
**Status:** Design v1

## Thesis
Clients remain when JS Solutions creates understandable value, communicates clearly, adapts priorities, and behaves as a trusted operator.

## Drivers
Progress, strategy, communication, reliable execution, realistic expectations, business-relevant reporting, continuous opportunity discovery, and low operational friction.

## Warning Signals
Declining engagement, unresolved access/delivery issues, no measurable progress, capacity problems, scope disputes, payment friction, leadership changes, poor lead quality, or expectation mismatch.

## Expansion
Expand where evidence supports adjacent SEO, conversion, locations, automation, paid acquisition, software, or other work.

## Metrics
Logo/MRR churn, retention duration, expansion, health, blockers, and evidence of value.

Clients should stay because the relationship is valuable—not because JS Solutions creates artificial dependency.
