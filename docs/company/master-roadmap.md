# JS Solutions + JS OS Master Roadmap

**Status:** Master alignment v1 — September 2026

## Purpose

Align the JS Solutions business roadmap with the JS OS technical roadmap so software development follows validated business needs rather than replacing business validation.

The governing relationship is:

```text
JS SOLUTIONS BUSINESS ROADMAP
          │
          │ proves what works
          ▼
      JS OS ROADMAP
          │
          │ systematizes and automates what works
          ▼
TECHNOLOGY-ENABLED GROWTH COMPANY
```

The business is the product being operated. JS OS is the operating system that progressively coordinates it.

Established local service businesses, initially in Texas, that:
- depend on local customer demand;
- have meaningful job/customer value;
- already have a legitimate operating business;
- have an existing website/Google presence;
- have identifiable visibility/trust/conversion opportunity;

# 13. Current experiment and immediate operating plan

## Acquisition

1. Keep initial first-touch cohort at exactly 50.
2. Monitor replies, bounces, opt-outs, and auto-responses.
3. Before every follow-up, read the live thread/state.
4. Execute Touch 2 only for eligible no-response prospects using new evidence/value.
5. Execute Touch 3 only for still-eligible prospects and close the loop.
6. Let the cohort mature.
7. Analyze the funnel and segments using the validation measurement system.
8. Decide whether another cohort is justified and what explicit hypothesis it tests.

No second cohort exists by default.

## Sales response

When a prospect engages:

**Response → classify → audit/conversation as appropriate → discovery → qualified decision → proposal only when justified → won/lost/nurture**

Do not rush positive replies directly to pricing/proposal.

## Fulfillment readiness

If a client closes:
- use the Client Growth System immediately;
- track actual onboarding and recurring hours;
- track direct AI/software cost;
- record baseline and first improvement;
- use the engagement as fulfillment validation data.

# 18. Decision gates before major automation

## Before Phase 7 Sales depth
Need enough acquisition evidence to know which sales workflow deserves automation.

## Before Phase 9 Client Operations depth
Need enough paying-client evidence to know recurring fulfillment patterns and economics.

## Before external communication autonomy
Need proven drafting quality, suppression/recipient safeguards, policy, approval history, and measured error risk.

## Before production deployment autonomy
Need bounded repositories/environments, tests/validation, approval/rollback controls, and proven execution history.

## Before financial autonomy
Need explicit financial policy, amount/vendor scope, reliable accounting/billing state, and strong owner controls. Some actions may remain human-only.

## Before company-level autonomy
Need proven department systems, useful CEO reasoning, reliable state, policies, cross-department handoffs, and measured economic benefit.

# 20. Final operating rule

JS Solutions and JS OS must evolve together, but they are not allowed to validate each other circularly.

A feature existing in JS OS does not prove the business needs it.

A business workflow being manually possible does not prove it should be automated.

The sequence is:

**Observe real work → define the system → measure it → validate value → encode controls → automate the useful parts → measure again.**

That is the path from a founder-operated service business to a controlled autonomous growth company.
