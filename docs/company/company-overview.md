# JS Solutions

**Status:** Draft operating definition (company facts below are known service scope, not a legal filing)

JS Solutions is the company. JS OS is its internal operating system. JS Growth is its customer-facing product platform.

## What the company does

JS Solutions provides:

- web development
- SEO
- local SEO
- digital marketing
- AI integration
- automation
- business growth systems

Do not treat this list as a complete legal service catalog. It is the operating scope JS OS should reason about.

## How the pieces fit

```text
JS Solutions     company
JS Growth        product (audits, prospecting, campaigns, …)
JS OS            internal command layer
```

See [system boundaries](../architecture/system-boundaries.md).

Development bootstrap writes one Organization row (`slug: js-solutions`, timezone `America/Chicago`). That is operating configuration in the JS OS database, not a legal filing.

Revenue figures, headcount, pricing, and legal entity details are not recorded in this repository and must not be invented here.
