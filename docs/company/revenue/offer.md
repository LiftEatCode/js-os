# Core Offer
**Status:** Hypothesis v1

## Working Offer
**Continuous Local Growth System**

## Outcome
Continuously improve the client's ability to generate qualified local demand through website, search visibility, reputation, and conversion improvements.

## First 30 Days
Business/market intake; access; website, search, GBP, reputation, conversion, competitor and measurement baselines; prioritized 30/60/90-day plan; highest-confidence quick wins.

## Recurring Work Pool
Technical/on-page SEO, service pages, useful local pages, internal links, metadata, structured data, performance, UX/CTAs, GBP, reviews, content improvement, competitive intelligence, analytics, lead tracking, conversion experiments, and selected automation.

Work is prioritized by expected impact—not arbitrary monthly quotas.

## Exclusions Unless Separately Scoped
Paid media spend, unlimited redesign/custom software, unlimited creative production, full-time social management, deceptive link schemes, fake reviews, guaranteed rankings/revenue, legal advice, or work without required access.

## Offer Ladder Hypothesis
1. Growth Audit / diagnostic
2. Foundation optimization project
3. Continuous Local Growth recurring service
4. Growth + Automation
5. Custom AI/software systems

## Capacity Rule
Every recurring deliverable needs a value rationale, estimated cost/time, repeatable SOP, and automation/delegation path where practical.
