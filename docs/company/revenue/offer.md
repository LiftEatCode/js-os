# Core Offer

**Status:** Sellable v1 — validate with real prospects before treating as product-market fit

## Primary Offer

**JS Solutions Local Growth System**

A managed growth engagement for established local service businesses. JS Solutions continuously diagnoses, prioritizes, implements, and measures improvements across the client's website, local search presence, reputation, and conversion path.

The client is buying an operating system for digital customer acquisition—not a quota of SEO tasks.

## Customer Outcome

Improve the probability that qualified local prospects:

1. discover the business;
2. trust the business;
3. contact or book the business;
4. can be measured well enough for future decisions to improve.

No ranking, lead-volume, or revenue guarantee is implied.

## Offer Architecture

### 1. Growth Audit — Acquisition Entry Point

**Price:** Free when used selectively for a qualified prospect; otherwise $295 standalone.

Purpose: diagnose the highest-value digital growth opportunities and determine whether a managed engagement is justified.

Includes:

- website and conversion review;
- local-search/SEO review;
- GBP review where public or authorized data is available;
- reputation/review signals;
- competitor comparison;
- measurement gaps;
- prioritized findings by impact, confidence, effort, and risk;
- concise recommended next actions.

A free audit is earned by qualification. It is not an unlimited public consulting service.

### 2. Growth Foundation — Fixed-Fee Implementation

**Working price:** $1,500–$3,500 one time.

Use when a business has significant foundational work but is not yet ready for continuous management, or when initial corrective work is large enough that folding it into the first retainer month would damage economics.

Potential scope:

- analytics/Search Console baseline;
- technical/on-page corrections;
- core service-page improvements;
- metadata/schema/internal linking;
- conversion-path and CTA improvements;
- GBP foundational optimization where access exists;
- review-system setup;
- priority tracking/measurement setup.

Exact scope is proposal-specific.

### 3. Local Growth System — Core Recurring Offer

**Working price:** $1,500/month for the initial single-location core offer.

Best for established local businesses that need ongoing optimization but do not require large custom development or high-volume content production.

Includes:

- monthly business/search/website review;
- prioritized growth backlog;
- technical and on-page SEO improvements;
- local SEO and GBP optimization where authorized;
- service/content improvements based on opportunity;
- internal linking/schema/metadata work;
- conversion and CTA improvements;
- reputation/review strategy support;
- competitor monitoring;
- analytics and lead/conversion measurement improvements;
- monthly business-focused review/report;
- implementation of the highest-priority work within the engagement's capacity.

There is deliberately no promise of a fixed number of posts, pages, keywords, or backlinks per month.

### 4. Local Growth Plus — Higher-Complexity Recurring Offer

**Working price:** $2,500/month starting point.

Use for clients with multiple service areas, larger sites, more aggressive growth targets, greater implementation volume, deeper competitive work, additional measurement requirements, or selected automation.

Possible additions:

- higher implementation capacity;
- deeper content/service-area development;
- conversion experiments;
- multi-location/local-market work;
- advanced reporting/attribution;
- workflow/marketing automation;
- more frequent strategic review.

Scope must remain bounded. Custom software is not implicitly included.

### 5. Custom Growth / Automation / Software

**Price:** Proposal-based.

Custom AI systems, business automation, major website rebuilds, integrations, custom applications, or unusually complex growth programs are separately scoped.

## First 30 Days of a Recurring Engagement

1. Business and market intake.
2. Access and ownership verification.
3. Website/technical baseline.
4. Search/local baseline.
5. GBP baseline where accessible.
6. Reputation baseline.
7. Conversion-path review.
8. Competitor baseline.
9. Analytics/measurement review.
10. Prioritized 30/60/90-day growth plan.
11. Highest-confidence quick wins.

If onboarding/foundation work materially exceeds normal recurring capacity, quote a separate Growth Foundation/setup project rather than silently absorbing unlimited work.

## Recurring Prioritization

Candidate work is prioritized using:

- expected business impact;
- confidence;
- effort;
- risk;
- urgency;
- strategic fit;
- dependencies;
- reversibility;
- revenue relevance.

## Exclusions Unless Separately Scoped

- advertising/media spend;
- unlimited website redesigns;
- unlimited custom development;
- unlimited pages/content;
- high-volume link acquisition;
- full-time social/community management;
- professional photo/video production;
- deceptive link schemes;
- fake reviews;
- guaranteed rankings, leads, or revenue;
- legal/compliance advice;
- work requiring access the client has not authorized.

## Commercial Guardrails

- Default to month-to-month recurring service after any agreed onboarding/setup obligation while the offer is being validated.
- Require 30 days' notice to cancel unless a proposal states otherwise.
- Client owns its website/content/accounts except for third-party licensed tools and JS Solutions proprietary systems.
- Scope is a prioritized capacity, not unlimited labor.
- Out-of-scope work is separately quoted or traded against existing priorities with explicit agreement.

## Validation Questions

For every early engagement record:

- Why did the client buy?
- Which part of the offer created urgency?
- What did they believe they were paying for?
- Which work consumed the most delivery time?
- Which work produced observable value?
- Did $1,500/month feel cheap, appropriate, or expensive to the buyer?
- What prevented expansion or retention?

The offer should evolve from this evidence.
