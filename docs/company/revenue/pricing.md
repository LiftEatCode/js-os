# Pricing Strategy
**Status:** Hypothesis; not a final public price list

## Objective
Price for value, delivery risk, access requirements, and sustainable margin.

## Working Planning Range
The ICP assumes many qualified recurring clients can support approximately $1,000–$3,000+ per month when value is demonstrated. This is a planning range, not locked pricing.

## Commercial Models
Use the model that fits the work:
- fixed-fee diagnostic/project
- setup/onboarding fee
- monthly retainer
- later software/platform fee
- custom AI/automation/software project

## Price Floor
**Collected revenue – labor – contractors – AI/API – software – other direct delivery cost = contribution margin**

Founder time counts as a real cost.

## Discount Rule
Prefer reducing/phasing scope over creating an unprofitable client. Founding-client discounts should only exist when learning/case-study value is explicit.

## Validate With
Close rate, objections, discount requests, delivery hours, direct costs, margin, retention, expansion, and realized client value.
