# Prospect Research Process

**Status:** Operating hypothesis v1

## Goal

Produce enough evidence to answer two questions before outreach:

1. Is this business worth contacting?
2. Can JS Solutions open with a specific, credible observation instead of generic agency copy?

## Research Order

### 1. Business Reality

Capture:

- business name;
- vertical/services;
- primary market/service area;
- apparent maturity;
- decision maker when legitimately discoverable;
- phone/email/contact channel;
- evidence the business is currently operating.

### 2. Website

Review:

- mobile usability;
- speed/performance signals where available;
- core service coverage;
- location relevance;
- titles/descriptions/headings;
- internal linking;
- structured-data opportunities;
- calls to action;
- phone/form/booking path;
- trust proof;
- broken/outdated information;
- obvious technical/indexability issues.

Do not claim a technical defect without evidence.

### 3. Local Presence

Where public or authorized information is available, review:

- GBP completeness/consistency;
- categories/services;
- review quantity/quality/recency patterns;
- photos/profile freshness;
- local relevance;
- obvious mismatch between website and listing.

Google's published local-ranking framework describes relevance, distance, and prominence as primary factors. Do not claim that any single optimization guarantees ranking.

### 4. Competitors

Select approximately three relevant competitors where possible and compare:

- service coverage;
- location coverage;
- website quality;
- trust/reviews;
- calls to action;
- content depth/usefulness;
- search-result presentation;
- obvious differentiation.

### 5. Opportunity Statement

Convert findings into 1–3 concise opportunity statements:

**Evidence → Business consequence → Recommended action**

Example:

> Your brake-repair page is difficult to find from the main service navigation and does not clearly target Magnolia. A competitor has a dedicated local brake page with stronger conversion actions. Improving the service architecture is a concrete opportunity to make this high-intent service easier for customers and search engines to understand.

Avoid fake dollar estimates unless supported by real client/business data.

## Research Depth by Tier

- **Tier A:** 15–25 minutes of manual/AI-assisted research before first contact; enough for a useful mini-audit.
- **Tier B:** 5–10 minutes to validate opportunity and personalize outreach; expand if the prospect engages.
- **Tier C:** minimal research until a buying signal improves priority.

These time budgets are hypotheses and should fall as tooling improves.

## Research Record

Capture:

- source/URL or evidence location;
- observation;
- confidence;
- opportunity category: Visibility / Trust / Conversion / Measurement / Competition;
- expected impact: Low / Medium / High;
- effort estimate;
- outreach-worthy? yes/no;
- date observed.

## Integrity Rules

- Never invent rankings, traffic, revenue, review counts, or competitor facts.
- Distinguish public evidence from inference.
- Do not present estimates as measured results.
- Do not use private/sensitive information for targeting.
- Do not bypass access controls or CAPTCHA.
- Public research is diagnosis, not permission to change external systems.
