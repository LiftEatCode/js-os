## If Prospect Says Yes

Send only the relevant Growth Opportunity Snapshot. Do not attach a proposal or pricing.

Then ask one simple question:

> If these are worth improving, is generating more of the right local customer demand something you're actively working on right now?

If yes, move to discovery. If no, record the reason and move to nurture or close the opportunity.

## If Prospect Asks Price Immediately

Do not hide pricing, but do not prescribe before discovery.

Suggested response:

> Our ongoing Local Growth System currently starts at $1,500/month, but I wouldn't recommend that just from a public website review. I'd first want to understand which services you want to grow, current capacity, what is already working, and whether there is enough opportunity to justify the investment. The short review I offered is free.
