# JLS Plumbing — Prospect Mini-Audit

## Executive diagnosis

JLS Plumbing has a strong owner-led trust foundation: Lance Siegmund has more than 30 years of plumbing experience and the business serves residential and commercial customers around Tomball. The owned website does not currently turn that experience into as strong a modern acquisition asset as it could.

## Priority opportunities

### 1. Turn owner experience into first-party trust proof
Evidence: the About page identifies Lance and details his long plumbing background.
Why it matters: local customers evaluating a plumber often need fast reassurance about competence and accountability.
Action: surface owner/master-plumber credibility, relevant qualifications, service guarantees where supportable, and customer proof closer to conversion points.

### 2. Build clearer high-intent service journeys
Evidence: the business supports multiple plumbing needs, while the site remains comparatively basic.
Why it matters: customers usually search by problem/service rather than by company history.
Action: create or strengthen dedicated pages around the most commercially valuable services and local search intent.

### 3. Modernize conversion paths
Evidence: current site presentation is older/basic despite substantial operating experience.
Why it matters: a dated or thin owned experience can undersell a credible business.
Action: improve page hierarchy, mobile calls to action, service-area clarity, proof placement and request-service paths.

## First 30-day direction

Validate target services and capacity, establish search/conversion baseline, prioritize the highest-value service pages, strengthen trust proof and calls to action, then measure resulting inquiry behavior.

No ranking, traffic, lead-volume or revenue claims are inferred from public evidence.
