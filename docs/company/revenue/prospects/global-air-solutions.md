## Evidence-backed opportunity

The business has a broader and potentially higher-value service mix than its owned website currently communicates through its acquisition architecture. The homepage lists substantial commercial HVAC and refrigeration capabilities, including preventative maintenance, walk-in refrigeration, food-service refrigeration, VRF systems and emergency repairs, but these capabilities are presented primarily as sections of one broad page rather than strong individual customer-intent journeys.

The owned site also repeats its About Us copy on the homepage and uses broad claims such as certified technicians, customized services and commitment to excellence without pairing those claims with much visible first-party proof in the crawled page.

This creates a credible opportunity to make the commercial/refrigeration specialization, owner-operated story and high-intent services easier to discover and evaluate.

## Primary sales angle

Global Air Solutions already offers a service mix that can support valuable residential and commercial work. The opportunity is to make the website communicate those capabilities with the same specificity customers use when searching — especially commercial refrigeration, preventative maintenance and emergency HVAC — while making the owner-operated trust story more visible.
