## Evidence-Backed Opportunities

### 1. Make Tom's owner-led trust story central to the acquisition journey

The testimonial archive contains unusually strong first-party material about Tom personally: honest diagnosis, avoiding unnecessary replacement, long-term relationships and customers seeking second opinions.

The main acquisition pages communicate generic company benefits more heavily than this owner-led proof.

**Opportunity:** Bring the real differentiator — Tom's reputation and repair/second-opinion philosophy — closer to service pages and conversion points.

**Priority:** High.

### 2. Tighten content quality around high-intent service pages

The site has substantial service/location content, but some copy is repetitive and heavily optimized around phrases such as `AC repair`, `Tomball`, and generic satisfaction claims.

**Opportunity:** Prioritize pages by actual service economics and search/customer intent, then improve them for clarity, proof, differentiation and conversion rather than publishing more volume for its own sake.

**Priority:** High.

### 3. Turn free second opinions into a clearer differentiated offer

The site advertises free second opinions, while testimonials specifically describe customers being told elsewhere that they needed replacements before Tom diagnosed a repairable problem.

**Opportunity:** If operationally sustainable, make second-opinion HVAC diagnosis a more deliberate conversion path with precise terms and customer proof.

**Priority:** High.

### 4. Reconcile public business-data differences

The current owned site uses 281-248-1969. Some third-party sources still display 281-257-3303 and differing business hours.

**Opportunity:** Audit major citations/directories and correct stale public information where the company controls or can request changes.

**Priority:** Medium.

### 5. Measure which services and markets actually produce profitable jobs

Before making ranking or lead promises, establish service/market demand, calls/forms, booked jobs and revenue attribution where feasible.

**Priority:** Foundational.
