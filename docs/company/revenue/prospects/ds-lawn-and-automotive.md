## Executive Assessment

D-S Lawn & Automotive is an unusually established local business with more than five decades in Tomball and two distinct revenue lines: automotive service and lawn/power-equipment sales/service. The mixed business model is a strength operationally, but the current owned website gives both lines similar weight and may dilute the automotive acquisition story.

The opportunity is not a generic redesign. It is to decide which side of the business should drive which customer journeys, then make the automotive portion do a better job of capturing high-intent repair demand.

## Evidence-Backed Opportunities

### 1. Separate the automotive acquisition journey from lawn-equipment shopping

The homepage rotates between lawn equipment and auto service, and the site's strongest content depth appears on the equipment side.

**Why it matters:** A person searching for brakes, alignment, A/C or diagnostics should land in an experience that immediately feels like an automotive repair shop, not a mixed retail/service catalog.

**Priority:** High.

### 2. Build stronger pages around valuable automotive services

The public site lists important automotive capabilities but gives them limited dedicated depth.

**Opportunity:** Prioritize the services D-S most wants more of — for example alignments, A/C, diagnostics, brakes or suspension — and build service-specific pages around customer problems, expertise, proof and next action.

**Priority:** High.

### 3. Use 50+ years in Tomball as stronger proof

Five decades of local operating history is a major trust asset.

**Opportunity:** Make that longevity more tangible through business history, team/service expertise, local customer proof and clear explanation of how the shop approaches diagnosis and repair.

**Priority:** Medium-high.

### 4. Clarify conversion actions by business line

The site uses broad `Get a Quote` calls to action across both equipment and automotive pages.

**Opportunity:** Create purpose-built actions for automotive customers — call for diagnosis, request service, ask about alignment/A/C/etc. — while preserving separate equipment shopping/service flows.

**Priority:** Medium-high.

### 5. Measure automotive demand separately

If the two business lines share a website and phone, measurement should distinguish automotive calls/leads from lawn-equipment demand before judging marketing performance.

**Priority:** Foundational.

## What We Should NOT Claim

- Do not say the mixed business model is inherently bad.
- Do not claim the business ranks poorly without location/query evidence.
- Do not assume automotive is more profitable than lawn equipment.
- Do not recommend separating brands/sites until economics and customer behavior justify it.
- Do not promise more repair work from a redesign.

## Locked Sales Angle

> D-S has more than 50 years of local trust, but the current website asks one customer journey to serve two very different businesses. The opportunity is to make the automotive side capture repair demand more intentionally without weakening the lawn-equipment business.
