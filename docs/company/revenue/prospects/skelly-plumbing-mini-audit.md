# Skelly Plumbing — Growth Opportunity Snapshot

**Prepared by JS Solutions**  
**Public-data prospect snapshot — not a full SEO engagement audit**

## The Short Version

Skelly Plumbing already has the fundamentals many local plumbing companies spend years trying to build: family ownership since 1990, a named master plumber, broad service capability and decades of local operating history.

The opportunity is not a generic website replacement. It is making those assets work harder when customers search for specific plumbing problems and decide which company to call.

## 1. Give High-Intent Plumbing Services Stronger Customer Paths

Skelly handles water heaters, under-slab leaks, gas work, water lines, repipes, stoppages and other meaningful services. Those customers arrive with very different problems and urgency levels.

### Opportunity

Identify the services Skelly most wants to grow and build deeper customer journeys around them: useful problem information, Skelly's relevant experience, trust proof, what to expect and a clear next action.

**Priority: High**

## 2. Make 35+ Years of Trust More Visible at Decision Points

`Family owned since 1990` and a publicly identified master plumber are strong trust signals. Public customer feedback also repeatedly highlights explanations, clean work and professional problem-solving.

### Opportunity

Bring that evidence closer to the service decision: owner/team credibility, verified customer proof, project examples and precise warranty/process information where applicable.

**Priority: High**

## 3. Use Real Business Data to Decide Which Markets to Grow

Skelly serves Tomball, Magnolia, The Woodlands, Spring, Cypress, Hockley, Waller and Northwest Houston.

### Opportunity

Instead of publishing thin pages for every city, determine which combinations of service + market create the best customers. Search visibility, calls and booked-job economics should determine where the next optimization work goes.

**Priority: Medium-high**

## Measurement Before Promises

This public review cannot see Skelly's private Search Console, analytics, Google Business Profile performance, call attribution, close rate or job revenue. Those should be established before anyone promises rankings or lead improvements.

## Bottom Line

Skelly appears to have a credible business and a reasonable digital foundation. That makes the growth question more valuable: which services and markets should the existing reputation be helping the company win more often, and where is the current customer journey leaving opportunity on the table?

**Suggested next step:** A short conversation about which plumbing services and geographic markets Skelly most wants to grow. That determines whether deeper optimization has enough economic upside to justify the investment.

---

Based only on publicly available information reviewed September 3, 2026. No private analytics, revenue, lead volume or internal operating data were used.
