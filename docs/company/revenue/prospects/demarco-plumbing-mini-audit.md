# DeMarco Plumbing — Growth Opportunity Snapshot

**Prepared by JS Solutions**  
**Public-data prospect snapshot — not a full SEO engagement audit**

## The Short Version

DeMarco Plumbing already has strong ingredients for local customer trust: 30+ years of experience, owner leadership from Responsible Master Plumber Kevin DeMarco, 24/7 emergency availability and meaningful residential/commercial service capability.

The immediate opportunity is not replacing the website. It is removing a few credibility leaks and making those existing strengths work harder when a customer is deciding whom to call.

## 1. Remove Ambiguous Homepage Trust Metrics

During the public review, the homepage displayed zero values for ongoing projects, completed projects and client satisfaction rate.

Those may simply be unused website counters rather than actual business statistics, but a customer has no way to know that.

### Opportunity

Remove them, configure them with supportable information, or replace them with stronger first-party proof such as licensing, years of experience, real project examples and verified customer feedback.

**Priority: High / Quick Win**

## 2. Put Kevin's Experience Closer to the Service Decision

The site explains that DeMarco is founded and operated by Kevin DeMarco, a Responsible Master Plumber with more than 30 years of experience. That's meaningful authority.

### Opportunity

Carry that credibility into the highest-intent service experiences. Someone deciding on a water-heater replacement, repipe, sewer problem or gas-line project should quickly understand who is responsible for the work and why DeMarco is qualified to handle it.

**Priority: High**

## 3. Develop the Existing Service Pages Instead of Publishing for Volume

DeMarco already has dedicated pages for important services including water heaters, drain stoppages, repiping, gas/sewer/water lines and leaks.

### Opportunity

Identify which services create the best customers and deepen those pages with real project proof, process information, useful FAQs, precise service-area relevance and stronger conversion measurement. The objective should be better customer acquisition, not simply more pages.

**Priority: High**

## 4. Treat Emergency Demand Differently

The site advertises 24/7 emergency availability. A customer with an active plumbing emergency behaves differently from someone researching a planned repipe or fixture upgrade.

### Opportunity

Make the emergency path extremely clear and measure emergency calls/booked jobs separately where practical so optimization can be tied to business outcomes.

**Priority: Medium-high**

## Measurement Before Promises

This public review cannot see DeMarco's Search Console, analytics, Google Business Profile performance, call attribution, close rate or job revenue. Those should be established before anyone makes ranking or lead-growth promises.

## Bottom Line

DeMarco already has a credible business story. The first opportunity is to eliminate visible trust friction and then make the company's experience and authority more persuasive on the service pages most likely to create valuable work.

**Suggested next step:** A short conversation about which plumbing services and markets DeMarco most wants to grow. That determines whether deeper optimization has enough economic upside to justify an ongoing program.

---

Based only on publicly available information reviewed September 3, 2026. No private analytics, revenue, lead volume or internal operating data were used.
