# Global Air Solutions LLC — Prospect Mini-Audit

Status: SALES DIAGNOSTIC
Date: 2026-09-03

## Executive diagnosis

Global Air Solutions has a stronger service story than the current website architecture makes immediately obvious. The company combines residential HVAC with commercial HVAC and refrigeration, has two named owner/operators and advertises 24/7 availability. The highest-value opportunity is not to invent a new brand story; it is to make the existing specialization easier for prospective customers and search engines to understand.

## Opportunity 1 — Build stronger high-intent service journeys

Evidence: The owned homepage lists commercial HVAC installation/repair, preventative maintenance, VRF, walk-in refrigeration, food-service refrigeration, emergency repair, residential HVAC and other services largely inside broad homepage sections.

Why it matters: A buyer searching for a specific problem or commercial capability should be able to land on a page built around that exact need, proof and next action.

Recommended action: Prioritize dedicated, useful service pages around the commercially valuable capabilities that match actual demand and company capacity, beginning with commercial refrigeration, commercial HVAC maintenance/emergency repair and the strongest residential services.

Impact: High
Confidence: High
Effort: Medium

## Opportunity 2 — Put the owner-operated trust story to work

Evidence: The site identifies Joseph Arispe and Joshua Arispe as Owner/Operators, but most of the homepage trust copy is generic language such as certified technicians, customized services and commitment to excellence.

Why it matters: Named local operators are more concrete and believable than generic contractor claims, particularly for a local service business competing on trust.

Recommended action: Integrate the owners, experience, licensing and actual operating approach into service and conversion pages rather than isolating that information near the contact section.

Impact: Medium-High
Confidence: High
Effort: Low-Medium

## Opportunity 3 — Replace generic/repeated content with proof and specificity

Evidence: The homepage repeats its About Us paragraph and contains broad claims that are not heavily supported by visible case studies, project examples, quantified proof or customer stories in the crawled page.

Why it matters: Commercial and higher-ticket buyers often need evidence that the company has handled work similar to theirs before making contact.

Recommended action: Replace redundant copy with first-party project proof, industries/equipment served, maintenance outcomes, customer examples and other evidence the business can substantiate.

Impact: Medium-High
Confidence: Medium-High
Effort: Medium

## First 30-day direction

1. Confirm which residential/commercial services produce the best customers and margins.
2. Establish measurement for calls/forms and service-page performance.
3. Prioritize 3–5 high-intent service journeys.
4. Strengthen owner/license/proof signals.
5. Remove repeated/generic copy where better evidence exists.
6. Measure resulting inquiry quality and iterate.

## Outreach angle

Global Air Solutions already has the service depth. The website opportunity is to make that depth — particularly commercial HVAC and refrigeration — easier to discover, trust and act on.

No ranking, traffic, lead-volume or revenue claims are made from public evidence alone.
