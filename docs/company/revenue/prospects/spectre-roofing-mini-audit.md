# Spectre Roofing — Prospect Growth Mini-Audit

Date: 2026-09-03
Purpose: sales diagnostic based only on public information

## Executive diagnosis

Spectre Roofing has useful raw trust assets — family-owned positioning, an owner with 20+ years of stated industry experience, BBB accreditation, and a clear Tomball-area operating presence. The primary opportunity is not to manufacture credibility; it is to translate that credibility and the company's roofing capabilities into clearer high-intent acquisition journeys on the owned website.

## Opportunity 1 — Build service-specific intent paths

**Finding:** The primary website navigation is Home, About, Products and Contact, while the homepage broadly discusses roofing, inspections/estimates and maintenance.

**Evidence:** Current official homepage/About navigation and copy.

**Why it matters:** A homeowner searching for a specific problem such as roof repair, replacement, storm damage or gutters should be able to immediately confirm that Spectre handles that need and understand the next step.

**Recommended action:** Prioritize dedicated pages for the highest-value services Spectre actually wants to sell, with problem-specific proof, process, FAQs, service-area relevance and a direct inspection/estimate CTA.

Impact: High
Confidence: High
Effort: Medium
Priority: 1

## Opportunity 2 — Make the owner story first-party proof

**Finding:** The site says the owner has more than two decades of experience but does not name him on the About page. BBB identifies Sean Little as CEO/Owner.

**Why it matters:** Roofing is a high-trust purchase. Connecting a real local owner to the experience claim can be stronger than anonymous company language.

**Recommended action:** Expand the About/trust layer with Sean's name, relevant experience, why Spectre was started, workmanship/process proof and authentic project/customer evidence.

Impact: Medium-High
Confidence: High
Effort: Low
Priority: 2

## Opportunity 3 — Verify local business-data consistency

**Finding:** Spectre's official website publishes 17350 State Hwy 249 Ste 220, while BBB currently publishes 27708 Tomball Parkway 276.

**Why it matters:** If one address is stale, inconsistent public business information can create customer confusion and complicate local-presence management.

**Recommended action:** Confirm the current customer-facing business address and normalize owned and major third-party citations where appropriate. Do not change anything until the correct business representation is verified.

Impact: Medium
Confidence: Medium
Effort: Low
Priority: 3

## Suggested first 30 days if engaged
1. Confirm target services, service areas and current business data.
2. Establish measurement baseline and conversion events.
3. Expand the highest-value service/customer-intent pages.
4. Strengthen first-party owner, project and customer proof.
5. Improve internal linking and local/service relevance.
6. Measure qualified calls/forms and iterate based on actual demand.

## Outreach thesis

Spectre already has the ingredients of a credible local roofing company. The website can do more to connect that credibility to the exact roofing problems customers are searching for.

No ranking, traffic, lead or revenue claims are inferred from this public-site review.
