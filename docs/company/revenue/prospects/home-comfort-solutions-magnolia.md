# Prospect Research — Home Comfort Solutions

**Date:** 2026-09-03  
**Vertical:** HVAC  
**Market:** Magnolia / Montgomery County, Texas  
**Stage:** QUALIFIED → QUALIFICATION_FIRST  
**Outreach status:** Not contacted

## Public business signals

- Home Comfort Solutions is publicly listed as a Magnolia-area HVAC contractor.
- Structured local-business data describes a newer company actively looking to build its customer base, with 24-hour availability and residential HVAC services.
- Public descriptions say the company is licensed and insured and serves a broad Montgomery County / north-Houston-area footprint.
- The visible structured listing has a very small review footprint compared with established competitors.
- A canonical owned website was not surfaced with enough confidence in this research pass.

## Opportunity hypothesis

Unlike Carter Automotive or Quality Air, this prospect may have an earlier-stage problem: **building the digital trust and local-visibility foundation while the company is actively trying to acquire customers.**

Potential work, if verified, could include:

- owned website/entity foundation;
- service and service-area architecture;
- GBP/local-search optimization where eligible and authorized;
- review-generation operating process;
- trust proof (license/insurance/team/service standards);
- emergency-service conversion path;
- lead measurement;
- prioritized content around profitable HVAC work.

## Main qualification risk

This company may be too early for the current $1,500/month ICP. A visible desire for customers does not prove budget, capacity, operating maturity or ability to retain a recurring growth engagement.

Therefore a large free audit is not justified yet.

## Decision

**QUALIFICATION_FIRST — do not spend Tier-A audit time yet.**

Before a deep audit, verify:

1. current owner/decision maker;
2. business age and operating maturity;
3. canonical website/domain;
4. actual service footprint;
5. capacity for additional work;
6. whether the economics can plausibly support the JS Solutions engagement.

If those signals are positive, this could be a valuable test of whether JS Solutions can help a younger HVAC company build a disciplined acquisition foundation. If not, nurture rather than discount the core offer.

## Research basis

Public structured local-business data and public web/search sources reviewed September 3, 2026. Private financial, analytics, lead and capacity data remain unknown.
