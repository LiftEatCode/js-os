## Executive Assessment

Skelly Plumbing is an established family-owned plumbing business with a credible local history, named master plumber, broad service capability and a modern owned website. This is not a `you need a website` prospect.

The opportunity is more sophisticated: translate 35+ years of operating history and customer trust into stronger service-demand capture, make first-party proof more visible, and establish which services/markets create the best economic return before expanding content.

## Evidence-Backed Opportunities

### 1. Build service-demand architecture around the work Skelly most wants

The site has a broad service catalog, but many important services are presented compactly. Water heaters, under-slab leaks, gas work, water lines and repipes represent different high-intent customer journeys.

**Opportunity:** Determine the highest-value/desirable services and build deeper pages/customer paths around those first, with useful problem information, process, proof and next action.

**Priority:** High.

### 2. Turn 35+ years of operating history into stronger first-party proof

`Family owned since 1990` and a named master plumber are substantial trust assets. Customer feedback also repeatedly highlights clean work and clear explanations.

**Opportunity:** Bring more of that evidence into service decision points: team/owner credibility, project examples, verified customer proof, code-conscious work, warranties where precise, and what customers can expect when Skelly arrives.

**Priority:** High.

### 3. Clarify pricing expectations without competing on price

Public feedback is mostly positive but includes some complaints about trip/minimum charges and perceived pricing for smaller tasks.

**Opportunity:** If operationally appropriate, improve expectation-setting around diagnostic/service-call structure and value. The goal is not to discount; it is to reduce surprise and strengthen perceived professionalism.

**Priority:** Medium.

### 4. Prioritize markets based on economics, not doorway-page volume

Skelly serves multiple Northwest Houston communities.

**Opportunity:** Use Search Console, GBP, call/job data and service economics to identify where demand and profitable work overlap, then strengthen genuinely useful local relevance around those markets.

**Priority:** Medium-high.

### 5. Establish acquisition measurement

Before claiming SEO improvement, establish source → call/form → booked job → service/revenue where possible.

**Priority:** Foundational.

## What We Should NOT Claim

- Do not say the current website is bad or outdated.
- Do not claim Skelly ranks poorly without query/location evidence.
- Do not characterize Skelly as cheap or expensive based on a small review sample.
- Do not promise rankings or lead volume.
- Do not create thin location pages solely because multiple cities are served.

## Locked Sales Angle

> Skelly already has the operating history and trust signals many plumbing companies try to manufacture. The opportunity is to make those assets work harder at the exact service and local-search decision points where customers choose whom to call.
