# Initial Vertical Prioritization
**Status:** Research-backed hypothesis v1  
**Decision date:** 2026-09-02

## Decision
Initial priority:
1. **Auto repair**
2. **HVAC**
3. **Plumbing**
4. **Roofing**
5. **Electrical**

Secondary: fencing, garage doors, pest control, landscaping/lawn care, remodeling/general contracting, pools.

## Auto Repair — Best Immediate Learning Market
JS Solutions already has direct operating experience, content/SEO knowledge, and client proof in auto repair. The category has strong local intent, urgent searches, repeat/LTV value, many independent operators, and frequently improvable websites/GBP profiles.

Risk: individual tickets can be smaller than major home-service replacement projects, so qualify for volume, capacity, and economics.

## HVAC — Best High-Value Expansion Vertical
HVAC combines local-search dependence, urgency, high replacement values, seasonality, and strong digital competition. One incremental replacement lead can have substantial economic value.

Risk: sophisticated incumbents and paid-media competition. Target strong established independents with weak digital execution.

## Plumbing — Urgency + Broad Service Surface
Plumbing offers recurring emergency demand, broad service coverage, local intent, repeat/referral potential, clear conversion actions, and many service/location opportunities.

Risk: lower-ticket jobs mean qualification should favor volume, higher-value services, and capacity.

## Roofing — Very High Ticket
Roofing offers high project economics, storm/local relevance, visual proof/review importance, and meaningful value from a small number of incremental wins.

Risk: low purchase frequency, seasonality/storm cycles, and aggressive lead-gen competition.

## Electrical — Trust + High Intent
Electrical is trust-sensitive and locally searched, with emergency work, upgrades, and meaningful projects. Reviews and professional presentation matter.

Risk: licensing/regulatory differences require accurate claims.

## Secondary Pool
Fencing has attractive project economics but generally less urgency. Garage doors have strong urgency but smaller average economics than major HVAC/roofing work. Pest control can have strong recurring LTV but requires different qualification/attribution. Other categories remain experiment candidates.

## Prospect Scoring
Keep separate dimensions for Fit, Opportunity, Economic Value, Capacity, Buying Signal, Contactability, and Proof Leverage.

## Initial Allocation
- 30% Auto repair
- 25% HVAC
- 20% Plumbing
- 15% Roofing
- 10% Electrical

This favors learning speed and existing proof rather than theoretical market size.

## Research Basis
The hypothesis is supported by Google's current local lead advertising ecosystem covering these service categories, current consumer project-cost research showing meaningful job economics in the home-service categories, and JS Solutions' existing auto-repair experience.

External evidence supports market attractiveness; it does not prove JS Solutions will close or retain clients. Sales experiments remain required.

## Decision Rule
Do not permanently niche based on this ranking. A preferred niche must demonstrate superior **demand → conversations → wins → contract value → margin → retention → measurable client value**.
