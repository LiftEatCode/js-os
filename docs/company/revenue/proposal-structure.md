# Proposal Structure

**Status:** Testable v1

## Principle

The proposal documents an already-understood business case. It should not be the first time the prospect learns what JS Solutions thinks their problem is.

## Recommended Structure

### 1. Executive Summary

Three short elements:

- what the business wants;
- what JS Solutions observed;
- the recommended engagement.

### 2. Current-State Findings

Use 3–5 priority findings from the audit/discovery process.

For each:

**Observation → Business consequence → Opportunity**

### 3. Objectives

Examples:

- improve visibility for priority services/markets;
- strengthen conversion from website visitors to calls/forms;
- improve local trust/reputation presentation;
- establish usable lead/conversion measurement;
- systematically close priority gaps against competitors.

Do not promise an unmeasured result as a guarantee.

### 4. Recommended Scope

For the core Local Growth System:

- continuous website/SEO/local-search review;
- prioritized growth backlog;
- implementation of highest-value work within engagement capacity;
- GBP/reputation optimization where authorized;
- content/service-page improvement when justified;
- conversion improvements;
- competitor monitoring;
- analytics/measurement improvement;
- monthly business-focused review.

Clarify material exclusions.

### 5. First 30 Days

List the baseline, access, measurement, 30/60/90 prioritization, and initial quick wins relevant to the prospect.

If a separate Growth Foundation project is required, state exactly why.

### 6. Investment

Example:

**Local Growth System**  
$1,500/month

Optional/required initial foundation:  
$X one time

State billing timing, cancellation notice, and any minimum term from the actual agreement.

### 7. Success Measurement

Choose metrics appropriate to the client:

- qualified calls/forms/bookings;
- conversion rate;
- priority service visibility;
- GBP/search engagement where reliable;
- review/reputation indicators;
- lead attribution quality;
- agreed business outcomes.

Avoid vanity metrics as the sole success measure.

### 8. Responsibilities

JS Solutions:

- execute agreed scope;
- communicate priorities/findings;
- protect credentials/data;
- measure and report honestly;
- request approval where required.

Client:

- provide timely access;
- maintain accurate business/service information;
- approve consequential changes when required;
- respond to leads;
- provide business context needed for prioritization.

### 9. Next Step

One clear action: approve/sign agreement and complete onboarding/payment steps.

## Proposal Rules

- no 20-page generic agency deck;
- no invented traffic/revenue forecasts;
- no fake urgency discounts;
- no hiding major setup work in vague scope;
- no unlimited-work language;
- scope must match the discovery conversation;
- price should not be a surprise if discovery was handled correctly.
