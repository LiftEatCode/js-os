# Positioning
**Status:** Draft v1

## Category
JS Solutions is a technology-enabled growth company for local service businesses.

## Core Positioning
> JS Solutions helps established local service businesses turn Google visibility and their website into a stronger, more predictable source of qualified leads and customers.

## Supporting Proposition
> We identify what is holding the business back online, prioritize the highest-impact opportunities, implement improvements, measure results, and keep optimizing.

## Model
Commodity agency: **Package → task quota → report**

JS Solutions: **Analyze → prioritize → improve → measure → repeat**

## Three Lenses
- Visibility: can the right customer find the business?
- Trust: does the digital presence make the business credible and preferable?
- Conversion: can interest easily become a lead/customer?

## Messaging Rules
Lead with outcomes and specific evidence. Explain technical issues in business language. Treat AI as infrastructure unless AI itself is the customer benefit.

## Never Promise
Guaranteed #1 rankings, instant SEO, fabricated certainty, guaranteed lead volume without a proven controlled offer, or arbitrary activity as proof of value.
