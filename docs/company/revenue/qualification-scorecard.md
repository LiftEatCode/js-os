# Prospect Qualification Scorecard

**Status:** Operating hypothesis v1

## Purpose

Prioritize businesses where JS Solutions has a credible opportunity to create measurable value and build a profitable relationship. The scorecard prevents the revenue engine from treating every business as an equally valuable prospect.

## Hard Disqualifiers

Do not score further when clear evidence shows:

- business is not meaningfully operating;
- no legitimate way to contact a decision maker;
- business cannot fulfill additional demand;
- business seeks deceptive/spam tactics;
- customer economics cannot plausibly support the engagement;
- business demands guaranteed rankings/leads as a condition of engagement;
- severe reputation/operational issues are expected to be concealed by marketing;
- JS Solutions lacks required competence or cannot obtain legitimate access.

## Scoring

Score each dimension from 0–5. Keep dimensions visible rather than relying only on the total.

### Fit — 0–5

5: established target vertical/local service business, strong geographic/service fit, owner-accessible, appropriate maturity.

3: reasonable local-service fit but one or more meaningful uncertainties.

0–1: materially outside current ICP.

### Opportunity — 0–5

5: multiple specific, credible visibility/trust/conversion gaps with meaningful upside.

3: several moderate opportunities.

0–1: digital presence already strong or insufficient evidence of worthwhile opportunity.

### Economic Value — 0–5

5: high-value jobs/LTV where a small number of incremental customers can justify the fee.

3: moderate ticket/LTV but sufficient volume/repeat economics.

0–1: economics make the engagement difficult to justify.

### Capacity — 0–5

5: clear evidence the business wants and can absorb more demand.

3: capacity unknown but business appears operationally healthy.

0–1: obvious staffing, scheduling, fulfillment, or reputation constraints.

### Buying Signal — 0–5

5: active expansion, hiring, marketing investment, recent site work, vendor dissatisfaction, explicit growth intent, or other strong signal.

3: normal operating business with no strong urgency signal.

0–1: evidence of contraction, closure risk, or no appetite for growth.

### Contactability — 0–5

5: owner/decision maker and legitimate direct contact path identified.

3: business contact channel exists but decision maker uncertain.

0–1: poor/uncertain contactability.

### Proof Leverage — 0–5

5: JS Solutions has highly relevant experience/case-study evidence for this business and its problems.

3: transferable evidence exists.

0–1: little relevant proof.

## Total

Maximum: **35**

Working priority bands:

- **28–35: Tier A** — research/audit first; personalized outreach.
- **22–27: Tier B** — worthwhile prospect; research before spending significant audit time.
- **16–21: Tier C** — nurture/low-cost research unless a strong new signal appears.
- **0–15: Do not prioritize** — usually disqualify or revisit later.

A high total never overrides a hard disqualifier.

## Evidence Quality

For every score, distinguish:

- observed fact;
- reasonable inference;
- unknown.

Unknown should not automatically become a zero. The purpose is prioritization, not fake precision.

## Initial Experiment Rule

For the first acquisition sprint, favor Tier A/B prospects in the current priority verticals: auto repair, HVAC, plumbing, roofing, and electrical.

Track score at discovery and eventual outcome. After enough data exists, determine whether individual dimensions actually predict conversations, wins, margin, and retention.
