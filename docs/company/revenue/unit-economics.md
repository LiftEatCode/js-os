# Unit Economics
**Status:** Measurement design

## Per Client
Track recurring/project revenue, founder hours, contractor cost, AI/API cost, software allocation, other direct cost, contribution margin, acquisition cost, retention, and expansion.

## Core Principle
Do not optimize for MRR at the expense of margin.

## Company Metrics
MRR/ARR, new/churned/expansion MRR, average revenue/client, contribution margin, CAC, estimated LTV/payback when enough data exists, owner hours/client, AI/software cost as a percent of revenue.

## Founder Time
Assign an internal cost even before the founder draws an equivalent salary.

## AI Budget
While revenue is small, keep general JS OS reasoning spend deliberately constrained; roughly $25–$50/month is an early planning range, excluding economically justified client-specific delivery costs.

Use cheaper models for extraction/classification and stronger reasoning for high-value decisions.
