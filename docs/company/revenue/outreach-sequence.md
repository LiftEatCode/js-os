# Outreach Sequence

**Status:** Testable v1

## Principle

Outreach is evidence-led and permission-oriented. The objective of the first message is not to sell a $1,500 retainer. It is to earn a conversation by showing that JS Solutions did enough work to identify a real opportunity.

## Primary CTA

> I found a few specific opportunities on your Google/website presence. Want me to send the short audit over?

Low friction. No calendar link in the first touch unless context strongly warrants it.

## Email Touch 1 — Observation

**Subject ideas:**
- quick idea for [Business]
- noticed this on [Business]'s site
- [service] opportunity in [market]

**Body structure:**

Hi [First Name],

I was looking at [Business] while researching [vertical] companies around [market].

I noticed [one specific, verifiable observation]. [One-sentence explanation of why it could matter].

I found a couple of other opportunities across the website/Google presence too. I can send you the short audit if you'd like — no pitch required.

— Josh
JS Solutions

## Touch 2 — Add Value

Send roughly 2–3 business days later when appropriate.

Hi [First Name],

One more thing I noticed: [second specific observation or competitor gap].

The reason I flagged it is [business consequence].

Happy to send the notes over if improving local visibility/leads is on your radar.

— Josh

## Touch 3 — Close the Loop

Send roughly 4–7 business days after Touch 2.

Hi [First Name],

I'll close the loop after this. I put together a few ideas for [Business] around [visibility/conversion/local search].

If growth through Google/the website becomes a priority, I'm happy to send them over.

— Josh

## Alternate Channels

### Phone opener

> Hi, this is Josh with JS Solutions. I was researching [vertical] businesses around [market] and noticed a couple of things on your website/Google presence that may be costing you opportunities. I'm not calling to sell you something on the spot — I wanted to see who handles growth/marketing there and whether it'd be useful if I sent the short audit over.

### Social/direct message

Keep it shorter:

> Hey [Name] — I was looking at [Business] and noticed a couple of specific website/local-search opportunities. I put a few notes together. Want me to send them over?

## Personalization Standard

A personalized first touch must contain at least one observation that could not be truthfully sent unchanged to 100 other businesses.

Bad:
> I help businesses rank higher and get more leads.

Better:
> Your AC repair page is linked from the footer but not the main service navigation, and the page doesn't clearly establish your Houston-area service coverage.

## What Not to Do

- no fake familiarity;
- no fabricated compliments;
- no fake urgency;
- no misleading RE:/FWD: subjects;
- no claims that JS Solutions found a problem when it did not;
- no guaranteed ranking/revenue claims;
- no automatic contact-form spam;
- no CAPTCHA bypass;
- no endless follow-up sequence;
- honor opt-outs/do-not-contact requests.

## Initial Test

Do not optimize around industry-wide cold-email benchmark claims. Establish a JS Solutions baseline.

First useful test: approximately 100 genuinely qualified, personalized outreaches, segmented by vertical and message angle.

Measure:

- delivery/bounce where available;
- positive response;
- audit accepted;
- conversation booked;
- proposal;
- win;
- explicit negative response;
- no response;
- time spent per prospect.

A low response rate is a diagnostic signal, not automatically a reason to send more volume.
