# Vertical Research Notes
**Research date:** 2026-09-02

## Purpose
Preserve the external basis for the initial ICP/vertical ranking without treating market research as internal fact.

## Google Local Lead Ecosystem
Google's current U.S. local-services documentation covers many initial target categories, including HVAC, plumbing, roofing, automotive repair-related categories, fencing, garage doors, pest control, landscaping/lawn care, and related services. Google's 2026 changes to selected U.S. local lead campaigns also explicitly include several home-service categories.

**Interpretation:** Google maintains specialized local lead products for these categories, supporting the hypothesis that local-search demand is commercially meaningful. This does not prove organic SEO/GBP work alone creates sufficient ROI.

## Project-Economic Signals
Current consumer cost research indicates especially strong high-ticket economics for HVAC replacement and roofing, with plumbing and electrical mixing urgent work with larger projects. Fencing and garage-door projects can also have meaningful economics; pest control may derive value from recurring service LTV.

These are rough market signals, not revenue promises or prospect-specific facts.

## Internal Strategic Signal
JS Solutions already has meaningful auto-repair domain experience through existing client work. That proof/learning advantage is why auto repair ranks first even though some home-service categories have higher average project values.

## Sources to Refresh
Before major niche/capital commitments, refresh:
- Google Local Services / local lead category and service-area documentation;
- Google Ads documentation for current local lead campaign behavior;
- current Houston/Texas project-cost and market data;
- JS Solutions' own funnel, contract, margin, retention, and outcome data.

## Evidence Rule
External research informs hypotheses. Real JS Solutions sales and client economics determine the niche.
