# Prospect Batch 001 — Northwest Houston

**Date:** 2026-09-02  
**Status:** Research batch; no outreach sent  
**Purpose:** Test the JS Solutions qualification/audit process against real local businesses before scaling prospecting.

## Research Boundary

This is a public-signal screen, not a full audit. Scores are provisional because revenue, capacity, budget, analytics, ownership intent, and internal lead handling are mostly unknown until discovery.

The initial batch intentionally favors independent auto repair and HVAC businesses around Magnolia, Tomball, The Woodlands, and Conroe. Franchise/large-chain businesses are generally deprioritized because owner accessibility and buying authority are weaker.

## Scoring Key

Dimensions: Fit / Opportunity / Economic Value / Capacity / Buying Signal / Contactability / Proof Leverage. Maximum 35.

Unknowns receive neutral rather than fabricated negative scores.
