# Ideal Customer Profile
**Status:** Research-backed hypothesis v1  
**Last updated:** 2026-09-02

## Primary Segment
Established local service businesses whose revenue depends on local discovery, reputation, calls, estimates, appointments, or booked jobs.

## Initial Geography
Texas, with Houston and surrounding communities favored for early learning, local familiarity, owner access, and case-study development.

## Initial Vertical Priority
1. Auto repair
2. HVAC
3. Plumbing
4. Roofing
5. Electrical

Secondary: fencing, garage doors, pest control, landscaping/lawn care, remodeling/general contracting, and pool services.

See [Initial Vertical Prioritization](vertical-prioritization.md).

## Why Auto Repair Is First
Auto repair is not necessarily the theoretically highest-value market. It is the best immediate learning market because JS Solutions already has direct domain experience and client proof, reducing sales friction while higher-ticket home services are tested.

## Strong-Fit Characteristics
- roughly 2+ years in business;
- established reputation and real demand;
- roughly 2–50 employees or equivalent capacity;
- meaningful average job value/LTV;
- existing GBP and website;
- enough reviews/proof to establish legitimacy;
- identifiable website/search/reputation/conversion weaknesses;
- competitors visibly outperforming it online;
- reachable owner/decision maker;
- capacity for additional leads;
- ability to invest roughly $1,000–$3,000+ monthly when value is demonstrated.

A rough $250k–$5M+ revenue band may help screening but is not a hard rule until validated.

## Disqualifiers
Pre-revenue firms, no-budget shoppers, chronic $100–$300/month buyers, businesses demanding guarantees, businesses unable to fulfill more demand, deceptive/spam requests, severe reputation problems expected to be hidden by marketing, or economics that cannot support the fee.

## Qualification Dimensions
Maintain separately: Fit, Opportunity, Buying Signal, Value Potential, Capacity, Contactability, Proof Leverage.

## First Experiment Allocation
- 30% auto repair
- 25% HVAC
- 20% plumbing
- 15% roofing
- 10% electrical

## Evidence Required
Track vertical, market, maturity, opportunity, channel, message, price, sales cycle, close rate, retention, margin, and client result. A preferred niche must earn that status through evidence.
