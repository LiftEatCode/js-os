# Discovery Call Playbook

**Status:** Testable v1

## Goal

Determine whether there is a valuable problem, whether JS Solutions is a fit to solve it, and whether both parties should invest time in a proposal.

Do not turn discovery into a 45-minute presentation about JS Solutions.

## Target Length

20–30 minutes for a normal local-service prospect.

## Opening

> Thanks for taking the time. I looked at the business before the call, so rather than run through a generic presentation I'd like to understand how you're currently getting customers, what you want to improve, and then I'll show you the biggest opportunities I found. If there's a fit, we can talk about what working together would look like.

## Questions

### Business

- What services are most important/profitable for you right now?
- What areas/customers do you most want more of?
- Are you trying to grow volume, higher-value jobs, a specific service, or a location?
- Do you have capacity for additional work today?

### Current Acquisition

- Where do most new customers come from now?
- How important are Google Search/Maps and the website?
- Are you running paid ads or working with another marketing provider?
- Do you know roughly how many calls/forms/bookings the website/Google presence generates?

### Pain / Gap

- What feels like it is not working as well as it should?
- What have you tried already?
- What would make marketing feel successful over the next 3–6 months?
- Is there a specific competitor you feel is beating you online?

### Decision / Readiness

- Who besides you is involved in a decision like this?
- If we identify a plan that makes business sense, is there anything preventing you from starting in the near term?
- Do you already have a marketing budget allocated, or would this need to be justified from expected business value?

Do not interrogate prospects or force every question when the answer emerges naturally.

## Audit Presentation

Present only the highest-value 3–5 findings.

For each:

1. Evidence — what was observed.
2. Consequence — why it matters to the business/customer journey.
3. Priority — why it is worth addressing now or later.
4. Approach — what JS Solutions would do.

Avoid overwhelming the owner with dozens of SEO defects.

## Offer Transition

> Based on what you've told me and what I found, I don't think you need a pile of disconnected marketing tasks. The biggest opportunity is to continuously work the highest-value problems across the website, local search and conversion path. That's what our Local Growth System is built around.

Then explain only the appropriate offer.

## Pricing Conversation

Core reference point:

> For a business like this, our core Local Growth System starts at $1,500 per month. If the initial foundation needs a larger rebuild or cleanup, I separate that into a one-time project instead of hiding it inside the monthly fee.

After stating price, stop talking and let the prospect respond.

## Qualification Outcomes

At the end assign:

- **Qualified — Proposal**
- **Qualified — Follow-up/Nurture**
- **Needs more information**
- **Not a fit**

Record why.

## No-Fit Standard

Do not send a proposal merely because someone accepted a call. A no-fit decision protects delivery capacity and improves sales data.
