# Sales Playbook v1
**Status:** Draft

## Philosophy
Diagnose before prescribing. Sales determines whether JS Solutions can create enough value for a mutually profitable engagement.

## Outreach
Use: specific observation → business consequence → evidence → low-friction next step. Avoid generic service lists.

## Audit Presentation
Lead with a few meaningful opportunities, not dozens of technical defects. Explain why each matters and what should change.

## Discovery
Understand growth goals, lead sources, high-value services, service areas, capacity, spend, prior vendors, lead handling, access, decision process, urgency, and customer economics.

## Qualification
Confirm need, fit, budget capacity, authority, ability to fulfill demand, realistic expectations, willingness to provide data/access, and acceptable working relationship.

## Proposal
Include current situation, objective, opportunities, scope, first priorities, measurement, responsibilities, exclusions, price/terms, and next step.

## Objections
Record categories: price, timing, trust, prior agency experience, guarantee requests, internal resource, unclear value, no capacity, incumbent vendor, absent decision maker.

## Follow-Up
Add value or clarify a decision. Stop on opt-out, disqualification, inappropriate fit, or exhaustion of the defined sequence. Move long-horizon fits to nurture.

## Learning
Record win/loss reason so targeting, offer, proof, and pricing improve.
