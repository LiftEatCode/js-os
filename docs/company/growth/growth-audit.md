# Growth Audit v1
**Status:** Commercial audit design

## Purpose
Diagnose where a business is losing digital growth opportunity and turn technical evidence into a prioritized business plan.

## Domains
### Visibility
Organic/local search, GBP, service/location relevance, crawl/indexing, content gaps.

### Trust
Reviews, proof, business-info consistency, credibility, expertise.

### Conversion
Mobile usability, CTAs, forms/calls, offer clarity, speed/friction, trust near conversion points.

### Competition
Service/content/review/positioning/local-visibility gaps.

### Measurement
Analytics, Search Console, call/form tracking, conversions, attribution gaps.

## Output
Do not lead with one vanity score. Emphasize:
1. opportunity
2. evidence
3. business consequence
4. recommended action
5. confidence
6. effort/risk
7. priority

Score, maturity, and confidence remain conceptually separate.

## Versions
Prospect audit: concise and sales-useful.
Client audit: deeper baseline for 30/60/90-day planning.

## Integrity
Never invent rankings, traffic, conversions, GBP facts, or competitor facts. Missing evidence is insufficient data, not success/failure.
