# Prospect Growth Audit

**Status:** Sales diagnostic v1

## Purpose

Give a qualified prospect a concise, evidence-based answer to:

> Where is this business most likely losing digital customer-acquisition opportunity, and what should be done first?

This is intentionally smaller than a full client baseline audit.

## Audit Domains

### Visibility

Review relevant evidence across:

- search/service coverage;
- local relevance;
- technical/indexability signals;
- service/location pages;
- metadata/content structure;
- GBP/public local presence where available.

### Trust

Review:

- reviews/reputation presentation;
- proof/testimonials;
- business identity consistency;
- certifications/experience claims where verifiable;
- website professionalism and clarity;
- competitor trust advantages.

### Conversion

Review:

- primary CTA;
- phone/form/booking path;
- mobile usability;
- service-page clarity;
- customer objections/friction;
- trust near conversion points;
- obvious speed/UX barriers.

### Competition

Compare approximately three relevant businesses where possible:

- service positioning;
- search-result presentation;
- service/location coverage;
- reviews/trust;
- website conversion;
- differentiation.

### Measurement

Identify whether basic attribution/analytics appear possible and what would need access to validate.

Never claim analytics findings without authorized data.

## Finding Format

Every finding should contain:

**Finding**  
What was observed.

**Evidence**  
Where/how it was observed.

**Why it matters**  
Potential customer/business consequence.

**Recommended action**  
What should change.

**Impact**  
Low / Medium / High

**Confidence**  
Low / Medium / High

**Effort**  
Low / Medium / High

**Priority**  
Now / Next / Later

## Prospect Deliverable

Keep the sales version concise:

1. Business + audit date.
2. One-paragraph executive diagnosis.
3. Top 3–5 opportunities.
4. Competitor snapshot.
5. Recommended first 30 days.
6. Invitation to discuss—not a manufactured emergency.

## Executive Diagnosis Template

> [Business] appears to have a solid [strength], but there are several opportunities where its digital presence is not fully translating the quality of the business into visibility and customer action. The highest-priority issues are [A], [B], and [C]. I would address them in that order because [brief business rationale].

## Scoring

Do not reduce the audit to one vanity grade. If category scores are used, display confidence/data completeness separately so missing data cannot masquerade as excellent performance.

## Sales Boundary

The audit should prove competence and create clarity. Do not give away an unlimited implementation plan or spend hours diagnosing a low-quality prospect.

Tier A prospects justify deeper personalization than Tier B/C prospects.

## Integrity

- Public evidence only unless authorized access exists.
- No invented ranking/traffic/revenue data.
- No guaranteed outcomes.
- No manipulative fear language.
- Distinguish observed problems from opportunities that require validation.
