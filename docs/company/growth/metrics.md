# Growth Metrics
**Status:** Dictionary v1

## Revenue
MRR, ARR, new/churned/expansion MRR, project revenue, pipeline value, collected revenue.

## Acquisition
Discovered, qualified, researched, audited, outreach prepared/sent, response/positive response, conversations, proposals, wins, close rate, time to close.

## Client
Active clients, average revenue/client, retention, churn, health, expansion, backlog, blockers.

## Economics
Contribution margin, CAC, cost-to-serve, founder hours, contractor/AI/software cost, estimated payback/LTV when enough evidence exists.

## AI
AI cost per qualified prospect/audit/client, tool failure, approval rate, rework, time saved, and attributable revenue where defensible.

## Guardrail
Never reward activity volume when it does not improve profitable revenue, retention, client value, or owner leverage.
