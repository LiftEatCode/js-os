# Business Model Validation Plan
**Status:** Active framework

## Question
Can JS Solutions repeatedly acquire, profitably serve, and retain customers without relying primarily on the founder's personal relationships?

## Gates
- ~$2k MRR: multiple-client proof
- ~$5k MRR: initial validation
- ~$10k MRR: repeatability

## Test Variables
Vertical, geography, qualification, audit, outreach channel/message/CTA, offer, price, setup fee, proposal, fulfillment.

## Diagnostic Threshold Hypotheses
Examples—not permanent laws:
- ~100 genuinely qualified outreaches with <3% response may indicate targeting/message/channel issues.
- ~20 qualified conversations with <10% close may indicate offer/trust/pricing/sales issues.
- Material early churn among the first several clients may indicate fulfillment/value/expectation issues.

Small samples require qualitative judgment.

## Change Conditions
Change when evidence repeatedly shows low willingness to pay, weak value, uneconomic sales cycles, unsustainable fulfillment, poor retention, or a clearly superior segment/channel.

## Automation Gate
Do not aggressively automate until inputs, decisions, actions, outputs, metrics, failure modes, and approval/risk boundaries are understood.
