# JS Solutions Business Model
**Status:** Draft v1

## Identity
- Operating brand: JS Solutions
- Planned legal entity: JS Growth LLC; formation is intentionally deferred while the model is validated.
- Website: js-growth.com
- Internal operating system: JS OS

## Objective
Build a profitable, repeatable, technology-enabled growth company for established local service businesses. JS Solutions sells improved customer acquisition and business growth, not disconnected SEO, website, social, or AI tasks.

## Operating Loop
**Diagnose → Prioritize → Improve → Measure → Repeat**

## Current State and Revenue Gates
Current recurring revenue is approximately $600/month from one client. That proves willingness to pay, but not repeatable acquisition.

- ~$2k MRR: multiple-client proof
- ~$5k MRR: initial business-model validation
- ~$10k MRR: repeatability
- $10k–$100k MRR: scale acquisition and delivery
- $1M+ ARR: durable operating company
- Long term: build toward a technology-enabled $100M company

These are management milestones, not forecasts.

## Customer
Initial hypothesis: established Texas local-service businesses with meaningful job/customer value, an existing web/Google presence, identifiable digital opportunity, reachable ownership, capacity for more demand, and roughly $1k–$3k+ monthly marketing capacity.

## Problem
Local businesses lose customers through:
1. Visibility — customers do not find them.
2. Trust — customers do not prefer them.
3. Conversion — digital attention does not become calls, forms, bookings, or sales.

## Positioning
> JS Solutions helps established local service businesses turn Google visibility and their website into a stronger, more predictable source of qualified leads and customers.

## Core Offer Hypothesis
A **Continuous Local Growth System** using the highest-value mix of website optimization, SEO, local SEO, GBP, reputation, content, conversion, analytics, competitive intelligence, and automation.

## Acquisition
**Discover → Qualify → Research → Audit → Outreach → Follow up → Conversation → Proposal → Won/Lost**

The audit is the diagnostic entry point, not the entire product.

## Fulfillment
**Onboard → Baseline → Prioritize → Execute → Validate → Measure → Report → Reprioritize**

## Economic Priorities
1. Profitable revenue
2. Client retention/value
3. Owner-time reduction
4. Safe autonomy

## AI Principle
Automate validated processes, not assumptions. AI should improve economics, speed, consistency, learning, or client outcomes.

## Assumptions Under Test
- Local service businesses are an attractive initial segment.
- Audit-led acquisition can create qualified conversations.
- Continuous optimization is more valuable than commodity task bundles.
- Clients will pay enough for healthy margins.
- Delivery can become repeatable and increasingly automated.
- The model can acquire clients outside the founder's personal network.

## Validation
The model is validated by repeatable customer acquisition, retention, contribution margin, manageable delivery, and evidence of client value—not by software completion.
