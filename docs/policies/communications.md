# Communications

**Status:** Draft operating definition. No email or messaging integration.

Sending messages to prospects, clients, or the public is a consequential action.

Until tools exist:

- Agents may at most draft (policy direction: `PREPARE`).
- Sending requires a future email/comms tool, permission check, and owner approval for outbound/external messages.

Internal JS OS notes and WorkItem descriptions are not outbound communications.

See [email integration](../integrations/email.md) and [approval policy](approvals.md).
